Content and brand voice

Derived from the live site's copy on 2026-11-23. Online Workwear is an Australian-owned, family-operated uniform and workwear supplier serving tradies, businesses and teams.

Tone

  • Straight and practical. Say what the garment does and who it suits. No hype words.
  • Trustworthy and local. Lead with Australian owned, Australian-based service, standing behind quality.
  • Helpful to teams. Make ordering for a business feel easy: no minimum orders, free embroidery set-up over a threshold.
  • Australian English (colour, organisation). Plain words; short sentences.

Copy rules

RuleExample
Button text says the action"Add to cart", "Choose colour", never "Click here".
Links say where they go"Hi Vis workwear", not "Read more".
Prices and offers are text"Spend $299 and save 10%". Thresholds come from theme settings, not a baked image.
Errors tell the fix"Enter an email like name@example.com", not "Invalid input".
Sentence case headings"Popular categories", not "POPULAR CATEGORIES" (use CSS only if the case is purely visual).
Spell out specs"UPF 50+ with mesh vent panels" keeps the live site's practical detail.

Imagery

  • Real people in real work settings, clothing clearly visible; hi-vis and flame-resistant ranges shown in use.
  • Consistent product shots on a plain background for grids, one crop ratio per grid.
  • Alt text describes the product (colour, style) for products, the meaning for promo images, and is empty for decoration.

Structured content

  • Keep the Organization JSON-LD from the live site; add Product and BreadcrumbList on product and category pages.
  • Reviews are real quotes with attribution; never fabricated or rewritten.

Decoration and branding copy (embroidery, printing) and marketing copy: second pass, separate briefs.
